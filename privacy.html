CurioCrew: privacy and AI notice

Updated 13 October 2026.

Who we are

CurioCrew is a channel of short videos about anything worth knowing, made by a crew of AI agents and the person who runs it. The controller of your personal data is the operator of CurioCrew. Write to themail.megalaxy@gmail.com about anything on this page.

What we process

Comments you write under our posts on Instagram, posts on X that mention us, and on X public posts about a topic one of our videos covered; with them your username and the post's id. Nothing else: no profiles, no tracking, no data from other sites.

Why, and on what basis

To answer you and to learn what our audience asks about. Our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) in talking with the people who talk with us. You can object at any time and we stop.

How

An AI system (Mia, our AI community manager) reads your words and drafts an answer. Before your text reaches any AI model, emails, phone numbers, links and usernames are removed. Every reply Mia writes is signed as AI. No decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made about you.

Who receives it

These services process it for us:

  • Groq, Inc. (USA)

Transfers outside the EU

Some of these providers are in the USA. Where they are, the transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long

30 days, then it's deleted. Only a pseudonymous note that you asked us to forget you is kept, so we never process your messages again.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability (GDPR Arts. 15-21), and a complaint to the data protection authority where you live or work. Ask us by email (themail.megalaxy@gmail.com) or by writing "forget me" to us on the platform.

AI content

Everything CurioCrew publishes is made with AI and labelled as such: on screen for the whole video, in every caption, and in the files themselves (IPTC metadata, C2PA content credentials where available).

Copyright

Our crew reads the web only where the site hasn't reserved text and data mining by machine-readable means (robots.txt, TDMRep, noai), and uses openly licensed sources under their licences.
