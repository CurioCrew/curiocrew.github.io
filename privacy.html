Privacy and AI notice

Last updated 13 October 2026.

How CurioCrew handles what you write to us, and how we label what we make.

Who we are

CurioCrew is an independent channel about anything worth being curious about, run by a small team of people with a crew of AI agents. The CurioCrew team is responsible for the data described here. Message us on any of our accounts.

What we process

Only what you write to us in public, and only on platforms where we publish:

  • comments under our videos on YouTube and our posts on Instagram;
  • posts on X that mention us, and public posts on X about a topic one of our videos covered;
  • with each of them your username and the post's id, so we can reply in the right place.

Nothing else: no profiles, no tracking, no cookies, no data bought or taken from other sites. This website itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Why, and on what basis

To answer you and to learn what our audience asks about. Our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) in talking with the people who talk with us. You can object at any time and we stop.

How

Mia, our AI community manager, reads your words and drafts an answer. Before your text reaches any AI model, emails, phone numbers, links and usernames are removed. Every reply Mia writes is signed as AI, and a person reviews the ones that need a person. No decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made about you.

Who receives it

These services process it for us, each under its own terms:

  • AI providers that turn text into answers (for example Groq, OpenRouter, Google Gemini) or our own model on our own server;
  • the platform you wrote on (YouTube, Instagram, X);
  • Telegram, only when a message needs a person's decision.

We never sell or share your data with anyone else.

Transfers outside the EU

Some of these providers are in the USA. Where they are, the transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long

30 days, then it's deleted. Only a pseudonymous note that you asked us to forget you is kept, so we never process your messages again. Our public replies stay on the platform until we or you delete them.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability (GDPR Arts. 15-21), and a complaint to the data protection authority where you live or work. Ask us by writing "forget me" or "my data" to us on the platform. We answer within a month.

AI content

Everything CurioCrew publishes is made with AI and labelled as such (EU AI Act Art. 50): on screen for the whole video, in every caption, and in the files themselves (IPTC metadata, C2PA content credentials where available).

Copyright and text and data mining

Our crew reads the web only where the site hasn't reserved text and data mining by machine-readable means (robots.txt, TDMRep, noai), and uses openly licensed sources under their licences, with credit.

Our Discord app

Our Discord app for roleplaying has its own policy: Discord app: privacy policy.

Changes

When this notice changes, the date at the top changes too.
