The terms for using our Discord app for roleplaying in a server.

The app

Our Discord app is a bot for roleplaying communities, run by the CurioCrew team: a duty clock, a leaderboard and contract reports. Using it in a server means accepting these terms and its privacy policy, alongside Discord's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Fair use

Don't try to fake duty time (for example by idling in voice or scripting joins), abuse the commands, or use the bot to harass anyone. The server's moderators decide what counts and may correct records.

Records and reports

Hours and attendance are a helpful record, not a guarantee: they depend on Discord, Bloxlink and Roblox answering. Contract reports contain an AI summary of the host's notes, marked as AI; the host is responsible for their notes.

Availability

We run the bot for free and may change, pause or stop it at any time. It is provided as it is, without warranties; to the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for lost records or decisions based on them.

Not affiliated

Contact
