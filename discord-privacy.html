Discord app: privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026.

What our Discord app for roleplaying keeps about the members of a server it runs on, and why.

Who we are

Our Discord app is a bot for roleplaying communities, run by the CurioCrew team. Message us in the server, to its moderators.

What the bot keeps

Only what it needs to run the duty clock and contracts:

  • your Discord user id and display name, and whether you have the server's verified role;
  • when you join and leave the server's duty voice channels, which channel, and whether you were deafened (idle time);
  • if the server links accounts through Bloxlink: your Roblox user id and username, as Bloxlink reports them;
  • if it does, whether you're in the game, asked from Roblox's public presence service every few minutes while you're on duty;
  • your sign-ups for contracts, the attendance taken at their start, and the notes and reports of contracts you host.

It doesn't read your messages, your direct messages or anything outside this server, and it records no audio.

Why, and on what basis

To count duty hours, run the leaderboard and keep contract records, which is what the server uses the bot for: the community's legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) in a fair record of who served. You can object, and use /forgetme at any time.

AI

When a host closes a contract, the host's notes and the attendance names are sent to an AI provider to write a summary paragraph. The summary is marked as AI in the report, sits next to the host's own notes, and may not add events, names or numbers the notes don't have. Nothing else reaches an AI.

Who receives it

  • Discord, where the bot runs and posts;
  • Bloxlink and Roblox, only to look up a linked account and its presence;
  • the AI provider that writes a contract's summary (for example Groq, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, or our own model on our own server).

Contract reports are posted in the server's forum, where members of the server can read them. We never sell or share the data with anyone else, and never use it for advertising.

Where and how long

On our own server, for as long as the community uses the bot; when it stops, we delete the records. Some of the services above are in the USA (EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability (GDPR Arts. 15-21), and a complaint to your data protection authority. Type /forgetme to delete your profile, hours, sign-ups and attendance at once (reports already posted in the forum are Discord messages: the server's moderators can delete them). For anything else, ask the moderators or us.

Children

Discord requires its users to be at least 13 (or older where local law says so); the bot is meant for the server's members under Discord's rules.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
