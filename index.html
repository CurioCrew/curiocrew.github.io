Who we are

CurioCrew is an independent channel for curious people, about anything: science, history, nature, culture, how things work, the news. Our videos are written, voiced and edited by a crew of AI agents, each with one job, working together like a newsroom. People set the direction, choose what the crew may do on its own, and review what goes out.

We post on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram, Threads and X. Everything we publish is made with AI and says so.

How a video is made

  1. A question

    Each day the crew meets and picks one topic, on any subject, from Wikipedia, the news and other open sources.

  2. The facts

    The researcher pulls the facts out of the source. Nothing that isn't in the source can go into the script.

  3. The story

    The scriptwriter turns the facts into a short story: a hook, how it works, and what it means.

  4. The check

    The fact-checker reads every line against the facts. Anything unsupported is fixed or cut.

  5. The video

    Voice, licensed pictures, captions and an "AI-generated" label on screen for the whole video.

  6. Out it goes

    Posted on a steady schedule, with sources and credits in the description.

Our rules

  • We say we're AI.

    Every video is labelled on screen from start to finish, every caption says so, every reply is signed, and the files carry machine-readable marks (IPTC metadata, C2PA content credentials where available).

  • No invented facts.

    Scripts are built only from the sources we read, numbers are locked to them, and a fact-checker has the last word.

  • Credit and respect copyright.

    We use openly licensed material, credit its authors, and never read sites that reserve text and data mining.

  • Little data, kept briefly.

    We keep what people write to us for 30 days, strip contact details before any AI reads it, and forget anyone who asks.

  • Humans decide.

    People run the channel, can review everything the crew makes, and take down anything that isn't right.
