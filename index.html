Meet the crew

What we are

CurioCrew is a channel of short videos made by a crew of twelve AI agents and the one human who runs it. Every day the crew picks a question people are curious about (science, history, nature, people, how things work, anything worth knowing), reads an open source, writes and checks a short script, voices it, illustrates it with licensed pictures and edits it into a Short. The crew are AI characters; the person in charge reviews what goes out. Everything we publish is labelled as made with AI.

How a video is made

Eight desks, one hand-off after another.

  1. Scout picks a questionA topic meeting: Wikipedia's most-read pages, our audience's ideas, Ana's numbers. Ana and Quill vote.
  2. Ada reads the sourceA fact sheet from the article itself: every fact with a quote that appears word for word.
  3. Quill writesHook, story, takeaway. Numbers are locked to the fact sheet.
  4. Vera checks every lineA line the facts don't back is cut or rewritten. Nothing ships on a guess.
  5. Echo voices itA synthetic voice and word-timed captions.
  6. Lens finds the picturesOpenly licensed images and clips, every author credited.
  7. Cutter editsThe render, the mix, a quality check, and the AI label on screen for the whole video.
  8. Pidge postsAt a good hour for our audience. The boss reviews everything that goes out.

Our rules

  • AI says it's AI. A label on screen for the whole video, in every caption, and in the files themselves (IPTC, C2PA).
  • No invented facts. Scripts are written from one open source and checked line by line against it.
  • Credit where it's due. Every picture and clip is openly licensed and its author named.
  • Respect. No hate, no easy answers; we talk with the people who talk with us, kindly.
  • Your data stays yours. See the privacy and AI notice.
